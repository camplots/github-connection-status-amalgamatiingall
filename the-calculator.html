HOW IT WORKS ↓

The Watts Better calculator analyses your actual energy profile and compares hundreds of solar and battery combinations against your consumption, electricity tariff and any existing system. It identifies the solutions that may deliver the strongest return on investment, greatest energy independence or fastest payback.

USE YOUR OWN ENERGY DATA

You can provide information in the way that suits you:

  • Upload an electricity bill as a PDF or image.
  • Authorise access to your interval electricity data.
  • Enter your consumption figures manually if you do not have a bill available.

The more detailed the information provided, the more accurately the calculator can model when your household uses energy, how much electricity it imports from the grid and where solar production or battery storage may create value.

COMPARE THE OPTIONS

The calculator models and ranks a wide range of system combinations, taking into account factors such as:

  • Your household’s consumption patterns.
  • Time-of-use tariffs and applicable charges.
  • Existing solar panels or battery storage.
  • Solar generation and self-consumption.
  • Battery capacity and likely usage.
  • Grid imports and potential energy independence.
  • Estimated savings, return on investment and payback period.

Rather than presenting a single generic recommendation, Watts Better shows how different system sizes and configurations could perform for your household. The underlying modelling process is designed to make an otherwise difficult comparison easier to understand.

RECEIVE A PDF REPORT

Once the analysis is complete, you can generate a personalised PDF report containing the key findings and ranked system options. This gives you a practical reference when comparing solutions, discussing equipment choices or deciding whether to proceed with solar, a battery or both.

A CLEARER VIEW OF EACH DECISION

Your analysis is designed to show more than one headline savings figure.

CURRENT PLAN

How does each solar or battery solution perform if you remain on your existing electricity plan?

NEW PLAN

How does the result change if you move to another plan or tariff?

COMBINED OUTCOME

What is the potential result when the system and electricity-plan change are considered together?

This breakdown helps prevent the value of a new system and the value of a plan change from being presented as though they were the same benefit.

COMPARE THE OUTCOMES THAT MATTER

Watts Better can help you assess solutions from different perspectives, including:

  • Estimated reduction in electricity costs.
  • Return on investment.
  • Estimated payback period.
  • Reduction in grid imports.
  • Potential energy independence.
  • Solar self-consumption.
  • Battery utilisation.
  • Exported energy and feed-in revenue.
  • The effect of your current electricity plan.
  • The potential effect of changing plans.
  • Future system expansion.
THE BEST OPTION

The best option is not necessarily the largest system or the one with the highest advertised savings. It is the configuration that is appropriately matched to your consumption, tariff, priorities and future plans.

HOW THE CALCULATOR WORKS

  1. Provide an understanding of your energy profile. Upload your bill, utilise interval data for greater accuracy, or simply enter your household’s consumption figures.
  2. We replicate your household energy profile so the Watts Better calculator can model consumption, time-of-use tariffs and the distribution of consumption; if you have existing solar, your feed-in tariff, level of export and current solar production; controlled loads, if in operation; and your daily supply charges.
  3. We then simulate an overlay of hundreds of different solar capacities, battery sizes and system configurations to quantify which combinations represent the best options when applied to your individual circumstances.
  4. We rank those solutions by what matters to you: strongest return on investment, fastest payback and greatest energy independence.
