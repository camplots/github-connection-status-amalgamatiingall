How energy flows through your home

A full day in about 90 seconds. Here is a short walk-through of what you are looking at.

  1. 1
    Solar fills the house first

    From about 7:30am the panels start producing, and the home runs on solar before anything else.

  2. 2
    Spare solar charges the battery, then goes to the grid

    Once the battery is full, the surplus is sold back. The tally on the right keeps count.

  3. 3
    After sunset the battery takes over

    The home runs on stored energy instead of buying from the grid.

Each moving token is exactly 1 kWh. Striped lines show where energy is flowing right now, and one second of video is about 16 minutes of the day. Sample numbers.
