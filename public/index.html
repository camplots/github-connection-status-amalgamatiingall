A QUOTE PROVIDES A PRICE, BUT WHAT'S THE BEST SOLUTION?

Most homeowners never really find out. They get a quote, see a payback period that sounds reasonable, and sign — without knowing whether a different system size, a battery, or simply a better electricity plan would have saved them more.

THAT’S NOT REALLY THEIR FAULT.

The software available to the solar industry doesn’t properly calculate savings even for a single system — let alone compare hundreds of possible combinations. So consumers end up confused, comparing quotes they have no reliable way to check.
